Cleaning that respects historic homes

Many Marblehead homes have wide pine floors, original woodwork, built-ins and old windows that do not like harsh products or rough tools. We adjust the way we clean to the house: gentle on old wood, careful around antiques and patient with the details that make your home one of a kind.

Living by the harbor means salt and sand

Life near the water is beautiful, but sea air leaves a film on windows and sills, and sand from Devereux Beach finds its way into every entryway. Our cleanings give extra attention to window sills, entry floors and mudrooms, where the coast likes to come inside.
