Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Owl Cleaning Co. ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects the information you share with us through this website, by phone, by email or by text message.

Information we collect

When you request a quote or contact us, we collect the information you provide: your name, phone number, email address, town, details about your home (bedrooms, bathrooms) and the service and frequency you want, plus any message you write. We also record the page you sent the form from and, when present, the advertising or campaign parameters in the link you used (for example UTM tags or a Google click ID), so we know how you found us.

How we use your information

We use your information only to respond to your request, prepare your quote, schedule and confirm appointments, provide customer support and improve our service. We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information.

Text messaging (SMS) program

Program: Owl Cleaning Co. customer care and offers texts. When you check the SMS consent box on our quote form, you agree to receive text messages from Owl Cleaning Co. at the phone number you provided about your quote request, appointment scheduling and reminders, service updates and occasional offers.

  • Message frequency varies based on your request and appointments.
  • Msg & data rates may apply according to your mobile carrier plan.
  • Opt out at any time: reply STOP to any message. You will receive one confirmation text and no further messages unless you opt in again.
  • Help: reply HELP to any message, call (978) 239-2635 or email hello@owlcleaningco.com.
  • Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchase. The SMS box on our form is optional and unchecked by default.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Mobile information is never shared

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. The categories of sharing described in the next section exclude your mobile number and your SMS opt-in data and consent.

Service providers

We work with a few service providers that help us run our business, such as our customer management, email and messaging platforms and our website host. They process information only on our behalf and only as needed to provide our services, and they may not use it for their own purposes.

Cookies and analytics

Our website may use basic analytics and advertising measurement tools to understand visits and form submissions. You can block cookies in your browser settings at any time.

How we protect it

Form submissions are sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) and stored in access-controlled systems. Only our team members who need the information to serve you can see it.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, update or delete your information at any time by calling (978) 239-2635 or emailing hello@owlcleaningco.com. To stop text messages, reply STOP.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version.
