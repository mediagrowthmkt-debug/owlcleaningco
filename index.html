FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What areas do you serve?

We clean homes in Marblehead, Swampscott, Lynnfield, Newburyport, Andover, Middleton, Topsfield, Gloucester, Rockport and Essex in Massachusetts, and we are opening our schedule in Salem and Hampton, New Hampshire.

Is Owl Cleaning Co. a maid service?

Yes. We are a local, owner-led maid service and house cleaning company. Whether you live in Marblehead, Swampscott, Lynnfield, Andover or Newburyport, our team is a short drive away and replies with a clear quote.

How much does a house cleaning cost?

A one-time cleaning starts at $170, biweekly cleaning starts at $150 per visit, deep cleaning starts at $340 and move-in or move-out cleaning starts at $300. The final price depends on the size of your home, number of bathrooms, condition and the services included, and you get a clear quote before anything is booked. Vacation rentals and Airbnb get a custom quote.

What if something was missed?

Let us know within 24 hours if anything was missed and we'll come back to fix it at no cost. Just call or text us with a photo and we schedule a time that works for you.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

No. Many clients leave a key or a door code. We follow your instructions for access and pets.

Which cleaning should I book first?

If your home has not had a professional cleaning in a while, start with a deep clean. Then a recurring plan keeps it that way in less time.
