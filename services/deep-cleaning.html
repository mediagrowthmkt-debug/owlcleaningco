Why start with a deep clean?

A deep clean resets the house. Once the built-up layer is gone, every regular visit after that is faster, cheaper and keeps the home looking better for longer. That is why we recommend starting here before a recurring plan.

New England homes need it more

Long winters keep windows closed, salt and sand come in on every boot, and spring brings pollen through every screen. A seasonal deep clean, especially in spring and fall, is the wise way to keep up with it.

How long does it take?

A deep clean usually takes longer than a standard visit, often four to seven hours depending on the size and condition of the home. For larger homes we may send two people so the job is done in one day.

FAQ

Deep Cleaning questions

How often should I get a deep cleaning?

For most homes, two to four times a year is ideal, usually at the change of seasons. If you are on a recurring plan, we can rotate deep cleaning tasks into your regular visits.

Do you clean inside the oven and fridge?

Yes, these are available as part of a deep clean. Just let us know in your quote request so we plan the time for it.

Is a deep clean more expensive?

It takes more time, so it costs more than a standard visit. Your quote will show the price up front, with no surprises when we finish.
