Which frequency is right for you?

Weekly is great for large families and homes with pets. Every two weeks is the most popular option and keeps most homes consistently clean. Monthly works well for smaller homes or as a helping hand between your own cleaning.

It starts with a deep clean

To get your home to a level that is easy to maintain, the first visit is usually a deep cleaning. After that, every recurring visit keeps it there, in less time.

A routine you can trust

Every visit follows your home's checklist and the notes you gave us: the dog that should stay inside, the vase that should not be moved, the room you would rather we skip. You tell us once and we remember.

Usually, yes. Because the home stays at a clean baseline, each visit takes less time. Your quote will show the price for each frequency.
