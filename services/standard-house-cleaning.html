What makes a standard clean different

A standard clean focuses on the surfaces your family touches and sees every day. It is not a quick wipe. We follow the same room by room checklist on every visit, so nothing depends on memory and nothing gets skipped because we were in a hurry.

How long does it take?

Most homes take between two and four hours, depending on size, number of bathrooms and how lived in the house is that week. We give you a time estimate together with your quote, so you can plan your day.

Standard or deep?

If your home has not had a professional cleaning in a few months, we usually recommend starting with a deep cleaning and then keeping it fresh with standard visits. It is the fastest way to get the house to a level that is easy to maintain.
