Book it at the right moment

The best time for a move out clean is after the furniture and boxes are gone. For a move in clean, before your things arrive. Tell us your moving date and we will find a slot that fits around the truck.

Landlord checklist ready

With no furniture in the way, we can reach every corner, inside every cabinet and along every wall. That is why a move clean is priced separately from a regular visit.

Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning questions

Does the house need to be empty?

It works best when it is. If a few items are still there, let us know and we will work around them.

Will this help me get my deposit back?

A professional move out clean covers what landlords usually check. We cannot control the landlord's decision, but we clean to the standard most leases ask for.

Can you clean the day of closing?

Often, yes. Tell us your closing or moving date as early as possible so we can reserve the time.
